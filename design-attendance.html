How it works:
  • Top table lists only the day's batches — change the date to see another day. Log attendance opens the roster modal.
  • The month calendar is per batch: class days follow the batch's schedule. Amber / "not logged" = a past class day with no record yet. Tap it to log that day's attendance.
  • In the modal all students start marked present; tap to mark absent. Save shows N present · M absent and turns the calendar day green.
  • Green days are already recorded — tapping opens them again to correct.
