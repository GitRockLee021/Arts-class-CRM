How batch assignment works: the Add Student modal gets a Batch dropdown (filled from these batches). When you save, that batch is recorded on the student's new enrollment. Batch is required on full save (like the course field); drafts skip it since they have no enrollment yet. The old free-text "Batch / schedule" box on the enrollment form is replaced by this dropdown.
