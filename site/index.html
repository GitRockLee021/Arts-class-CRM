The Pravaha way

A foundation, not a hobby class.

Learners move through a carefully sequenced curriculum. Each level builds the skill the next one depends on, so progress is visible — in technique, confidence and independence.

01

Observation before style

Students learn to see proportion, form, light and composition. Style grows from looking closely, not from copying.

02

Media, introduced properly

Pencil, pen, oil pastel, colour pencil, soft pastel, watercolour, gouache, acrylic and oil painting — each with its own discipline.

03

Confidence through making

Regular finished work, exhibitions and age-appropriate challenges help students trust their own creative voice.

The curriculum

Six levels, one clear path.

Every learner begins with the fundamentals and progresses at their own pace. The level names come from Tamil — a seed, a sprout, a bud, a flower.

See all levels & fees
  • Varnam — colour, play and creative confidence for young beginners.
  • Vidhai — the foundation: observation, drawing and colouring technique.
  • Thulir — composition, visual storytelling and mixed media.
  • Arumbu — perspective, human anatomy, shading and advanced rendering.
  • Malar — independent work across watercolour, acrylic and gouache.
  • Kani — portfolio-level practice, large works and exhibition preparation.
