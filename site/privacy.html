Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and protect personal information at Pravaha Art Space.

Effective 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website and the Pravaha Art Space programme are operated by Right & Left Learning Academy, No. 2, Agaram Main Road, Indra Nagar, Selaiyur, Chennai 600 073, Tamil Nadu, India. For privacy questions, contact us at rightleftlearningacademy@gmail.com or +91 91766 56580.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Student and parent details: name, age or date of birth, phone number, email address, residential area and emergency contact.
  • Enrolment details: programme level, batch, class attendance, fee plan and progress notes.
  • Payment details: the amount paid, date, fee type, receipt number and Razorpay payment or order reference.
  • Communications: messages sent by phone, WhatsApp, email or through the website, including enquiry details.
  • Website information: basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in section 7.

We do not collect or store full card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs or net-banking passwords. Those are entered directly on Razorpay’s secure checkout.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, arrange trial classes and manage admissions.
  • To deliver classes, track progress and communicate about schedules, attendance and academy events.
  • To calculate fees, issue receipts and send fee reminders.
  • To process payments and handle refunds, duplicates or payment disputes.
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.
  • To improve our programmes, website and parent communication.

4. Payment processing

Online payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited and its banking partners. When you open a Razorpay payment link or checkout, Razorpay receives the information needed to process the payment, including your name, contact details and payment method. Razorpay handles that information under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation of the payment, a payment reference and limited payment-method details (such as UPI or card) so that we can issue a receipt and reconcile fees.

Our fee-reminder messages are delivered through WhatsApp, operated by Meta. The parent or guardian’s phone number and message content are processed by Meta to deliver the message.

5. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with:

  • Payment processors such as Razorpay, to collect fees and process refunds.
  • Messaging providers such as WhatsApp/Meta, to send class updates and fee reminders.
  • Service providers who host our websites, databases or communication tools, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Professional advisers, auditors or authorities where required by law, or to protect our legal rights.
  • A successor or buyer if the academy or its business is transferred, subject to equivalent privacy protection.

6. How long we keep information

We keep student and enrolment records for as long as the student is enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards for alumni communication and dispute resolution. Payment and receipt records are kept for the period required by applicable tax and accounting law. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or anonymised.

7. Cookies and website analytics

This website is a simple informational site. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not run third-party advertising. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files. If we add analytics in future, this policy will be updated.

8. Children’s privacy

Most of our students are children. We collect a child’s information from a parent or guardian, or with their consent. Parents and guardians may contact us to review, correct or request deletion of their child’s information, subject to our record-keeping obligations.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, encrypted connections and restricted staff access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration or loss.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your child.
  • You may ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • You may ask us to delete information that we are not required to keep.
  • You may ask us to stop sending promotional or reminder messages. We may still send essential service messages about classes and payments.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 1. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published here with its effective date. Material changes will be communicated to enrolled families where practicable.
