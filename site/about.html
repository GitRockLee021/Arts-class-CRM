About the academy

Art taught with patience, structure and care.

Pravaha Art Space is the fine arts unit of Right & Left Learning Academy in Selaiyur, Chennai. We believe creativity is not a talent some children are born with — it is a skill that can be taught, practised and trusted.

Our approach

Learning begins with drawing — observation, proportion, form and composition. As students progress, they explore a wide range of traditional and contemporary media: pencil, pen, oil pastels, colour pencils, soft pastels, watercolours, gouache, acrylics and oil painting.

Each level builds on the previous one. That sequencing matters: it gives learners a real foundation instead of a series of disconnected craft activities. Small batches let the teacher correct, encourage and stretch each student individually.

What we care about

  • Technical foundations taught properly, at the right age.
  • Patience and observation over shortcuts and copying.
  • A calm studio where mistakes are part of the work.
  • Finished work, exhibitions and the confidence that comes from both.
Founder & mentor

Mrs. Radha Kannan

Artist · Fine arts educator · Founder of Pravaha Art Space

Radha Kannan has been teaching fine arts since 2019, mentoring more than 500 students across India and Australia. She holds a Diploma in Fine Arts (DFA) from The Tamil Nadu Dr. J. Jayalalithaa Music and Fine Arts University and an Advanced Certification in Painting from Dessin Academy. She is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) at William Carey University, Shillong.

She also holds an M.Sc. in Psychology, which shapes a learner-centred, patient classroom for children of different ages, temperaments and abilities. As a practising contemporary artist, her abstract and modern works have been shown at Lalit Kala Akademi (New Delhi), Cholamandalam Artist Village (Chennai) and CP Art Centre (Chennai), and one work is in a private collection in New York.

Her vision for Pravaha Art Space is a centre of excellence where artistic learning is joyful, meaningful and transformative for learners of every age.
