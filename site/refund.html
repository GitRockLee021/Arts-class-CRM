Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when fees can be refunded, how to request a refund, and how long it takes.

Effective 10 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to fees paid to Pravaha Art Space, a unit of Right & Left Learning Academy, whether paid online through Razorpay or at the studio. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Cancellation before the batch starts

If you cancel an enrolment at least 24 hours before the first class of the batch, the tuition fee paid will be refunded after deducting any non-refundable payment-gateway charges. The one-time admission fee is not refundable once admission is confirmed. A kit that has already been issued or opened is not refundable.

3. After the batch has started

Monthly tuition fees are non-refundable once the batch has started. This includes cases where a student stops attending, withdraws mid-month, or changes to a different batch or level. Where the academy agrees to a transfer, the unused portion of a prepaid fee may be adjusted against future classes at the academy’s discretion.

4. Missed classes

Fees are not reduced or refunded for classes a student misses. A missed class may be made up in another suitable batch, subject to seat availability and prior notice. Make-up classes are not guaranteed. Classes missed without notice may not be eligible for a make-up class.

5. If the academy cancels a class

If the academy cancels a class and cannot offer a replacement or make-up class, the equivalent fee for that class will be adjusted against the next month’s fee or refunded on request. If the academy discontinues a programme entirely, the unused portion of fees paid in advance will be refunded.

6. Duplicate, failed or incorrect payments

  • If a payment is debited twice for the same fee, the duplicate amount will be refunded after verification.
  • If a payment fails but money is debited, it is normally reversed automatically by the bank or payment provider. If it is not reversed, contact us with the payment reference and we will follow up with Razorpay.
  • If you believe you have paid the wrong amount, contact us before the next fee cycle so we can adjust or refund the difference.

7. How to request a refund

To request a refund, contact us with:

  • The student’s name and the programme or batch.
  • The receipt number or Razorpay payment reference.
  • The date and amount paid.
  • The reason for the request and your contact details.

Email: rightleftlearningacademy@gmail.com

Please send refund requests from the phone number or email address used at enrolment where possible. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing a request.

8. Processing time and method

Approved refunds are usually processed within 5–7 working days of approval. Refunds for online payments are returned to the original payment method through Razorpay; the time for the amount to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider. Cash or studio payments may be refunded by bank transfer. Payment-gateway charges, where non-refundable, will be deducted.

9. Non-refundable items

  • One-time admission fees after admission is confirmed.
  • Art kits that have been issued or opened.
  • Fees for classes already attended or for a month that has already started.
  • Payment-gateway charges that the academy cannot recover.

10. Chargebacks and disputes

If you have a concern about a payment, please contact us first so we can resolve it directly. Raising a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us may delay resolution. We will cooperate with Razorpay and the bank in investigating any genuine dispute.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page, with its effective date, applies to new requests. Changes do not apply retrospectively to refunds already approved.
