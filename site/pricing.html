How payment works. Monthly fees are payable in advance. The academy shares a secure Razorpay payment link with the parent or guardian on WhatsApp; you can also pay at the studio. Razorpay accepts UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. A receipt is issued for every payment. See the refund & cancellation policy.

What the monthly fee covers

The fee is for structured teaching and studio time, not merely supervision.

  • A sequenced curriculum with clear level-by-level goals.
  • Small-batch teaching with individual correction and feedback.
  • Guidance across drawing, colour and painting media as the level requires.
  • Periodic progress feedback to parents or adult learners.
  • Opportunities to exhibit and present finished work.

Good to know

  • Admission and kit charges are one-time and apply at joining, where listed.
  • Consumable art materials are replenished by the student as needed.
  • Fees are reviewed periodically; any change is communicated in writing in advance.
  • Missed classes are not refunded, but a make-up class may be arranged subject to batch availability.
  • Fees can be paid monthly or for multiple months in advance.
